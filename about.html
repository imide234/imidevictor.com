My Digital Marketing Journey

My passion for digital marketing began in 2019, when I started helping small businesses create and optimize their Google Business Profiles.

I quickly realized that visibility alone was not enough. Businesses needed structured strategies to convert attention into real customers.

By 2021, I expanded my expertise into:

  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

  • Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
  • Search Engine Marketing (SEM) & Paid Advertising
  • Website Development & Conversion Optimization

This evolution allowed me to move beyond isolated tactics and begin building complete digital growth ecosystems for businesses.
